The point of view

Honey should not cover rum. It should complete it.

Aurora was created around restraint. Real honey is used as a finishing signature—softening the spirit, rounding the profile, and leaving depth without an overly sweet taste.

The craft

Every element has a purpose.

01

A composed rum base provides structure, warmth, and the foundation for Aurora's final character.

02

Honey brings texture and roundness while remaining integrated, polished, and deliberately restrained.

How to serve

Pour it with intention.

Aurora is designed for slow moments—served neat, over one large cube, or used as the signature spirit in a refined cocktail.

01

Neat

Serve in a clean rocks glass and allow the honey finish to open naturally.

02

Over Ice

One large cube creates a slower, cooler, and softer pour.

03

In Cocktails

Pair with citrus, coffee, ginger, tropical fruit, or bitters.
