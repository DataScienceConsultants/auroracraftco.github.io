Our Story

Aurora Craft Co was born from a simple idea: create something familiar in an entirely new way.

We bring together the warmth and character of rum with the natural richness of honey to create a spirit designed to be experienced slowly, shared freely, and remembered.

Our approach is rooted in curiosity and craft. Every element—from the balance of the spirit to the way honey transforms its aroma, texture, and finish—is considered with purpose.

The result is Aurora: a honey-finished rum with its own identity.

Smooth. Rich. Unexpected.

But Aurora is about more than what is inside the bottle.

It represents the moment when an idea becomes something real. The experimentation. The late nights. The conversations. The adjustments made one small detail at a time. The belief that something different deserves to exist simply because no one has made it quite this way before.

Like the first light that appears on the horizon, Aurora represents the beginning of something new.

And this is only the beginning.

Crafted with curiosity. Finished with honey. Made to be remembered.
